Square’s Generative AI Terms say “you own your AI Content”. The same terms then give Square a licence to it that is “irrevocable” and “perpetual”, for “any reason”.[^1] You keep ownership. Square gets the right to use, copy, adapt and publish it, worldwide, alongside you. Its third-party providers “may also use your data to improve their services”, and you may not use what comes back to improve yours. Using any Square AI feature means accepting all of it.

## What Square takes when you use its AI

What counts as “AI Content”? Everything, in both directions. The terms define Input as “any text you type or images, content, or data you upload into AI Products”, and Output as what those products generate. Square’s licence over both is “a worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free, fully-paid, transferable, irrevocable, perpetual, and sub-licensable right to use, reproduce, modify, adapt, publish, prepare derivative works of, distribute, publicly perform, and publicly display your AI Content”, in any media, “for any reason”.[^1]

In plain terms: Square may adapt or publish what your staff type in, and may pass that right to others.

The terms offer one way to refuse it: “IF YOU DO NOT WISH TO GRANT SQUARE A LICENSE TO ANY OF YOUR AI CONTENT, PLEASE DO NOT USE SQUARE’S AI PRODUCTS.”[^1]

## Follow one prompt through Square’s AI

1. You enter an Input.

2. Square may share it: “any Input you provide, including any personal data you choose to include within that Input, may be shared with such third party service providers”.[^1]

3. Those providers “may also use your data to improve their services”.[^1]

4. The Output comes back to you. Square holds the licence above over both.

5. You may not use any “content, data, Outputs or other information received or derived from AI Products, to directly or indirectly create, train, test, or otherwise improve any machine learning algorithms or artificial intelligence systems”.[^1]

Step 3 is the only one that reaches beyond Square, to providers the terms do not name.

## Square has not said which features count

Which Square features are AI Products? Square’s General Terms say the AI Terms apply when you use “any Generative AI or AI-powered products, features and tools”.[^2] Neither document lists them, and Square’s two Australian pricing pages name no AI product and no AI price.[^3][^4]

So for any feature, the question is whether what you feed it counts as data you “upload into AI Products”. Section IV is wider, because it reaches anything “received or derived from AI Products”. Whether your own exported sales and guest records fall inside it, Square would have to say.

## For your AI lead

My read, in order:

1. Keep dietary and allergy notes out of every Square AI feature until Square confirms in writing. Section II bars any “sensitive personal data” in an Input, and its list includes “health data”.[^1]

2. Keep rostering and applicant screening out of Square’s AI. Section I bars using AI Products to “Make decision impacting the terms or conditions of hiring or employment”.[^1]

3. Train your own models only on records you export from your own systems, never on what Square’s AI produces. Keeping that export is [your job under Square’s other terms](/writing/your-square-data/).

## What this does not show

- Which Square features are AI Products today, and what they cost. The pricing pages I read list none.

- Whether the providers’ use is limited elsewhere. Section V says “your use is subject to the terms of those third parties”, and I did not read those terms.[^1]

- Which providers, or in which countries. The terms name none.

- Whether section IV reaches your own sales and guest records.

Square’s written answer to the email below would settle the last three.

## Put this in writing

Send this to Square before anyone on your team switches on a Square AI feature, and keep the reply.

> Subject: Your Generative AI Terms and our account
>
> Your Generative AI Terms (effective 15 February 2025) apply to your AI Products. Please confirm in writing:
>
> 1. Which Square products and features are AI Products today, and what does each cost per location?
> 2. Which third-party providers receive our Inputs, in which countries, and on what terms may they use our data to improve their services?
> 3. Does section IV, on model training, cover our own exported sales and guest records, or only content produced by your AI Products?
> 4. Can we use an AI feature without granting the licence in section II?
