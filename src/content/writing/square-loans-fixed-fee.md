Pay off a Square loan in six months instead of twelve and you pay the same fee, so each year of borrowing costs about twice as much. That is my arithmetic, and it follows from Square’s own words: the fee “will never change regardless of how quickly or slowly the loan is repaid”.[^1] Neither page I read gives the fee in dollars. Repayments come out of your daily card sales, “in addition to Square’s processing fees”.[^1]

## One fee, however fast you repay

How is the price set? Square: “There are no ongoing loan interest charges. You’ll pay one fixed loan fee to borrow the loan, which is the difference between the total owed amount and the initial loan amount.” It adds: “The cost of your loan does not change by making prepayments.”[^1] In plain terms: the fee is the whole price, agreed up front.

The Loans page puts it this way: “Pay just one loan fee. This fee never changes and is paid back over the life of the loan. No late fees, ongoing interest or surprises.”[^2]

Square says its loans are “designed to be paid back within 12 months”.[^1] Its example of repayment: “if you bring in $1,000 one day and your repayment rate is 16%, you pay off $160 of your loan.”[^2]

## From the offer to the 18-month deadline

1. Square makes an offer from your card sales: “custom loan offers based on your unique sales data”.[^2]

2. You borrow an amount and agree the fixed fee.

3. Each day, “A fixed percentage of your business’s daily card sales is automatically deducted and applied toward your loan balance”. The rate “can’t be adjusted once your loan is accepted”.[^1]

4. If sales do not cover the minimum of 1/18th of the initial balance every 60 days, Square debits your linked bank account.[^1]

5. At 18 months the rest is due: “you’ll only pay down half your total loan balance in 18 months. You’ll owe the remaining balance upon the loan maturity date.”[^1]

My read: Square’s design target is 12 months. Repay in 6 and the same fee costs about twice as much per year. Stretch to 18 and it costs about a third less per year. That is my arithmetic, assuming even repayment.

## The loan is tied to your Square sales

Who can borrow? To apply, “Your business must process payments on Square for a minimum of five days before submitting your formal application.” Eligibility includes “consistent and continuous payment card processing through Square”, and the page says you must “make $250,000 or more in sales per location”.[^2]

The loan “is tied to the specific account location that applied for the loan”. If you take payments on another Square account, those sales “don’t automatically go towards the repayment of your loan”.[^1] Paying the loan off has a cost of its own: “We can’t guarantee that you’ll receive a new offer after you make an additional payment or pay off your loan.”[^1]

My read: the loan is repaid from, and depends on, your card sales through Square. That is a reason to keep processing there. I did not read the loan agreement, so I cannot say what happens if you move.

## For your AI lead

My read, in order:

1. Take the fee in dollars from your offer.

2. Divide it by the average amount you will owe and by the years you will owe it. If you repay evenly, the average owed is roughly half the loan.

3. Do the sum for 6, 12 and 18 months. The answer differs each time, and the fee does not.

4. Build a view of card sales by account and location. Repayment follows the location tied to the loan, so that view shows what is paying it down. Your own [monthly Square export](/writing/your-square-data/) has the rows you need.

## What this does not show

- The fee, in dollars or per cent, for any offer. Square says it “depends upon payment card processing history, loan amount and other eligibility factors”.[^2]

- The loan agreement. “Terms and conditions apply”, and I did not read them, so default and acceleration terms are unknown.

- Whether the help article is current. It shows no date.

- How other vendors’ lending compares. I did not check them.

## Put this in writing

Send this to Square before you accept an offer, and keep the reply.

> Subject: Our loan offer: the numbers in writing
>
> Please confirm in writing for our offer:
>
> 1. The fee in dollars, the total we will owe, and the repayment rate.
> 2. The payoff date at our current card sales, and what we owe at 18 months if we pay only the minimums.
> 3. How repayment treats sales on other Square accounts and locations.
> 4. What the loan agreement lets Square vary or end if we move card processing.
