On Lightspeed’s own Australian fee table, a venue putting about $100,000 a month in card sales through another processor would pay $490 a month for the choice: $5,880 a year, my arithmetic.[^1] The Service Agreement says an eligible customer “must use Lightspeed Payments unless otherwise agreed to by Lightspeed in writing”, and charges the fee “for each month in which Customer processes transactions through such service”.[^2] A second clause, for Restaurant POS (U-Series), names a flat “$/€/CHF 99.00 per month”. The agreement does not say which one you pay.

## Use another processor and a monthly fee applies

When does the fee apply? Clause 5.3 covers you if you use the Products, are in a country where Lightspeed Payments is offered, and are “eligible for Lightspeed Payments”. Clause 5.4 then sets the price by volume: the fee “shall be calculated based on Customer’s estimated average monthly transaction volume”. On an annual subscription, “this monthly Fee will be assessed upon renewal”.[^2]

In plain terms: every month you process cards elsewhere carries a fee, and on an annual plan it lands at renewal.

Lightspeed’s plain-language column beside Section 5 reads: “Your Order Form will tell you how often to pay your Fees.” It does not mention a processor.[^2]

## The fee table: $75 to $1,160 a month

Clause 5.4 points to a “Fee table linked here”. For Australia it is a one-page PDF with ten bands in Australian dollars. At $7,140 to $28,569 of estimated monthly volume the fee is $75. At $214,290 to $249,999 it is $1,160. Above $250,000 the table says “Please Contact your Account Manager”.[^1]

The table starts at $7,140. It does not say what applies below that.

The PDF shows no date and adds that figures will be “subject to fluctuations in exchange rate from time to time”. Its table is an image, so I transcribed the figures by hand and checked them against the image again on 17 November 2026.[^1]

## Then a second clause says 99.00

In Part II, under Lightspeed Restaurant POS (U-Series), clause 2.2 says: “Customer will be assessed a Fee in the amount of $/€/CHF 99.00 per month for each month in which Customer processes a transaction through such Third-Party Services provider”.[^2] That is a flat 99.00 in a currency the clause shows as “$/€/CHF”. Nothing in the agreement says whether a restaurant on U-Series pays the table fee, the 99.00, or both.

Lightspeed Payments itself is “a separate payment processing service governed by a different set of agreements”, so its processing rates are not in this document.[^2]

## How the fee reaches you, step by step

1. You use Lightspeed and are eligible for Lightspeed Payments.

2. The agreement says you must use it unless Lightspeed agrees otherwise in writing.[^2]

3. In any month you process transactions through another service, a monthly fee applies.[^2]

4. The fee follows your “estimated average monthly transaction volume”, read against the table.[^1]

5. On an annual subscription, the fee is assessed at renewal.[^2]

## For your AI lead

My read, in order:

1. For each venue, pull a month of card volume processed outside Lightspeed Payments. The clause sets no minimum, so any month with such transactions counts.

2. Find each venue’s band in the table.

3. Add it up for the group. Ten venues at about $100,000 a month each would pay $4,900 a month, or $58,800 a year (my arithmetic, using the $490 band for $92,860 to $107,139).

4. Put that total beside what Lightspeed Payments would cost you. This agreement does not give those rates.

The same agreement lets Lightspeed [hand your contract to a buyer without asking](/writing/lightspeed-assignment/).

## What this does not show

- Which of clause 5.4 and clause 2.2 applies to a restaurant, or whether both do.

- The currency of the 99.00 figure.

- Who decides eligibility for Lightspeed Payments, who estimates your volume, and how often.

- The date of the fee table, and what applies below $7,140 a month.

- What Lightspeed Payments costs, so no comparison is possible from these documents.

Lightspeed’s written answer to the email below would settle the first four.

## Put this in writing

Send this to Lightspeed before you sign or renew, and keep the reply.

> Subject: Payments clause 5.3 and the fee under 5.4
>
> Your Service Agreement (last updated 26 February 2026) says in clause 5.3 that we must use Lightspeed Payments unless you agree otherwise in writing. Please confirm in writing:
>
> 1. Are we eligible for Lightspeed Payments, and who decides?
> 2. If we process any transactions through another provider, which fee applies each month: the Transaction Fee under clause 5.4, the “$/€/CHF 99.00 per month” in clause 2.2 for Restaurant POS (U-Series), or both? In which currency?
> 3. What volume figure sets our band, who estimates it, and when is it reviewed?
> 4. Will you agree in writing to let us use another provider, and at what fee?
