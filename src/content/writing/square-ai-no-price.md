A ten-venue group on Square for Restaurants Plus, with inventory, loyalty, marketing and mobile POS, pays at least $3,970 a month, my arithmetic from Square’s own price list.[^1] Every one of those prices is per location. Square AI, which Square now advertises to Australian businesses as “built into your everyday tools”, appears on that list nowhere. Its own page gives no price either, and carries the label “Beta”.[^2] What it will cost per venue, Square has not said.

## The plan and the main add-ons are priced per location

How does Square charge a group? By the venue. The restaurant pricing page lists Plus at “$129/month”, “per location + processing fees”, and mobile POS at “+$50/mo. per location for locations using mobile POS”.[^1] Most add-ons follow the same rule:

- Square Restaurant Inventory by Marketman: “+$149/mo. per location”.

- Square Loyalty: “Starting at $49/mo. per location”.

- Square Marketing: “Starting at $20/mo. per location”.

- Square Kiosk Lite: “$45/mo. per device”.

My arithmetic: $129 + $149 + $49 + $20 + $50 is $397 a month per location. Ten locations is $3,970 a month, or $47,640 a year. Loyalty and Marketing are “Starting at” prices, so that is a floor.

## Square AI: a page, a beta label, no price

What does Square say its AI does? Its Australian page: “Square AI is built into your everyday tools to pull game-changing insights from your data and handle routine tasks to help you get ahead.”[^2] You can “Ask anything about your business”. It “brings in web data so you can ask about local weather, events, news and reviews”, and offers to “Generate content faster” for item descriptions, marketing campaigns and team announcements.

The page carries the label “Beta”. It shows no price, no plan, and no claim that the AI is free.

The only mention of AI on Square’s pricing overview is in the footer: “Some content may have been translated using AI to help serve our global community.”[^3]

## The terms are set, even if the price is not

Square’s General Terms say its Generative AI Terms “apply when you use any Generative AI or AI-powered products, features and tools” offered by Square.[^4] Those terms take a perpetual licence to what you put in, and Square AI is described as working on “your data”. [What Square’s AI terms let it keep](/writing/square-ai-terms/) covers the licence clause by clause.

## How Square tells you a price has moved

Square has changed a price recently, and the terms update is where it said so. The Payment Terms that took effect on 1 October 2026 list among “The key changes” “an increase to the Instant Transfer fee from 1.5% to 1.95%”.[^5] On a $5,000 transfer that is $75 before and $97.50 after, my arithmetic.

My read: if Square prices AI the way it prices its main add-ons, a ten-venue group pays it ten times, and the first notice may be a terms update. Ask now, while there is nothing to renegotiate.

## For your AI lead

My read, in order:

1. Find out which Square AI features are switched on for your account, venue by venue.

2. Before staff build a weekly routine on a beta feature, decide what you would do if it were priced or withdrawn.

3. Budget AI per location. When a price arrives, multiply it by your venues before you compare it with anything else.

4. Keep Square AI’s inputs inside your own data rules, because the Generative AI Terms apply to what goes in.

## What this does not show

- What Square AI will cost, or whether it stays free while it is in beta. No page I read says.

- Which Square AI features work for Australian restaurants today. The page lists features without saying which are live here.

- Square for Restaurants Premium pricing, which the page gives only as “Custom pricing available”.

- Whether Square treats Square AI as an AI Product under its Generative AI Terms. The General Terms suggest so, and Square has not said so for this product.

Square’s written answer to the email below would settle all four.

## Put this in writing

Send this to Square before any venue relies on Square AI, and keep the reply.

> Subject: Square AI: features, price and terms for our account
>
> Your Square AI page (squareup.com/au/en/ai) describes Square AI as a beta, with no price. Please confirm in writing:
>
> 1. Which Square AI features are available to our account in Australia today, and which are in beta?
> 2. What will Square AI cost per location per month when the beta ends, and how much notice will we get before charging starts?
> 3. Is Square AI included in Square for Restaurants Plus or Premium, or priced separately?
> 4. Do your Generative AI Terms apply to Square AI, and which of our data does it use?
