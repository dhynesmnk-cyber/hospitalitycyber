## Short history

Perpetual licences and local servers gave way to monthly subscriptions in the 2010s. App stores and free tiers made trials easy. Vendors then bundled payments, banking and lending to make leaving costly. Consolidation since 2024 shows the direction: American Express completed its purchase of Tock in October 2024,[^1] DoorDash agreed to buy SevenRooms in May 2025,[^2] Thoma Bravo agreed to buy Olo in July 2025,[^3] and Lightspeed sold its Upserve product line in April 2026.[^4]

## Now

**Contract traps seen in the market**

- Payment bundling: the POS price assumes you use their payments, and a third-party processor attracts an extra fee. Lightspeed's Australian service agreement says an eligible customer must use Lightspeed Payments unless Lightspeed agrees otherwise in writing, and charges a monthly fee for each month a customer processes through another provider.[^5]

- Auto-renewal with 60 to 90 day notice.

- Price rises tied to transaction volume, not features.

- Data export limited to PDFs or reports, not records.

- Support included on the cheap tier only by email.

## Next three years

- Vendors add AI tiers priced per seat or per outcome. Check what is truly included.

- Fewer vendors per venue as suites absorb point tools.

- Breach disclosure and data location clauses become negotiable (chapters 04 and 05).

- Marketplaces and integration fees shift cost to the venue.

## Accommodation vs retail

- **Accommodation:** the PMS sets the rest of the stack. Check API access and integration fees before signing.

- **Venues:** the POS sets payments, reservations and loyalty.

- **Retail bev:** the ecommerce platform sets shipping, tax and marketing.

## A short buying checklist

1. All-in price for 36 months, including payments, hardware, support and integrations.
2. Exit: how to export all data, in what format, and how fast.
3. Where data lives and who can access it.
4. Breach notice and liability limits.
5. Two reference customers of your size and type in Australia.
6. A named person for escalation, and the support hours that matter to you (Friday night, Sunday).

## Risks

- Free trials that turn into annual contracts.

- Shadow subscriptions on a personal card.

- Choosing a vendor because a friend did, not because it fits.
