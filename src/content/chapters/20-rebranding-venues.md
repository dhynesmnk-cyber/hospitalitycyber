A rebrand is a change of name, look and promise. The risky part is not the logo. It is everything the old name is attached to.

This is a background chapter. It draws on industry practice and has no sourced figures, so everything below is my read.

## Where the old name lives

List every place the name appears and give each an owner: your Google Business Profile, booking and delivery platforms, review sites, social handles, domain and email, POS receipts, supplier accounts, licences and insurance.

## What to check first

- **Legal.** Business name registration, trade mark search and registration, liquor and food licences in the new name, ABN and GST details, and signage approvals. I did not research the rules for any of them, so check each with the relevant body or your adviser.

- **Reviews.** A rename on a listing may keep or reset review history. Check before you change the entity.

- **Hotels.** Changing a franchise brand changes the property system, distribution, loyalty and reservations, and may carry a fee to leave. Read the exit terms first.

- **The website.** Keep the old domain redirecting to the new one for as long as you can.

## The order I would work in

1. Check the trade mark and domain before the name is final.

2. List the places the old name appears and assign owners.

3. Update licences and insurance before the day.

4. Tell regulars first, by email, SMS and in the venue. Then the media.

5. Ask whether a new menu and price position would do more than a new name.

## Questions to put in writing

Send these to your booking, delivery, website and POS vendors.

1. Can the venue name be changed on our account without losing our history, reviews or data?

2. How long does it take, and what does it cost?

3. Will the old name continue to appear anywhere, such as receipts or confirmation emails, after the change?

## What this chapter does not show

- Trade mark, business name or licensing rules.

- What a rename does to reviews or search ranking on any platform.

- Whether a rebrand lifts trade. I have no data.
