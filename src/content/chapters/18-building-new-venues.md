The Australian Building Codes Board released a preview of the National Construction Code 2025 in February 2026, and the full code was to be published on 1 May 2026 and become available for adoption by the states and territories straight away.[^1] New South Wales and Queensland have said they will defer adopting it until May 2027, and will make variations whose detail was not yet known.[^1]

A venue’s fit-out is designed to a code, so which version applies depends on the state. This chapter reads what I could verify about the code, the cash rate and the licensing risk. I could not source construction costs or lead times, so I give none.

## Which code applies

The trade report quotes the NSW government saying deferral “will provide the construction industry with more time to adjust to the changes”.[^1] I did not confirm the position in other states, or read the code itself. Ask your certifier which edition applies to your site and when.

## The cost of money

On 3 November 2026 the Reserve Bank lifted the cash rate target to 4.85 per cent, and said inflation is not expected to reach the top of its target range until late 2027.[^2] A build is a loan before it is a business. Chapter 21 covers funding.

## What I take from it (my read)

- A new venue is a construction project, a licensing project and a technology project that all have to finish on the same day. Licence timelines vary by state and council, so do not set an opening date until the liquor licence is granted.

- Specify technology at design stage: data cabling and Wi-Fi access points on the plan, POS, kitchen screen and printer positions with power and network, cold room sensors, CCTV and access control, and enough electrical capacity for future equipment.

- Budget for working capital in the first months of trading, not only for the build.

- Ask the lender and the insurer what they want at handover, such as a register of connected devices.

## Questions to put in writing

Send these to your certifier, builder and lender.

1. Which edition of the National Construction Code applies to this site, and from what date?

2. What are the fixed price, the contingency and the programme, and what changes if the liquor licence is late?

3. Which technology items are in the build contract and which are ours to buy?

4. What does the lender need at completion and at handover?
